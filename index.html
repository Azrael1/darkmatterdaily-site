What it does

Prepared publishing for approved science videos

DarkMatterDaily Publisher helps prepare, review, and upload approved short videos for the DarkMatterDaily channel. The app is intended for controlled publishing workflows where human review remains part of the approval process.

The system may connect to supported publishing platforms, including TikTok, to upload approved video files, associated captions, metadata, and publishing status information.

TikTok uploads are separated into two clear actions: draft upload, where the video is sent to TikTok for later review in TikTok, and Direct Post, where the authorized user confirms the caption, privacy, interaction settings, disclosures, and approved MP4 before automatic posting.

TikTok connection

Controlled account authorization

DarkMatterDaily Publisher uses TikTok OAuth so an authorized account owner can connect TikTok without sharing a password. The app requests only the permissions needed to identify the account and upload or post approved short videos.
